INVESTIGATION PROTOCOL

HOW A MEDIA DETECTIVE WORKS

01

OBSERVE

Examine the information carefully before accepting the claim.

02

QUESTION

Ask who created the information and why it was created.

03

VERIFY

Look for evidence and reliable sources that support the claim.

04

THINK

Evaluate the information before believing or sharing it.

MEDIA & INFORMATION LITERACY

MIL FIELD GUIDE

📚 MIL QUICK GUIDE
🔎

SOURCE

Who created the information? Is the source identifiable and credible?

🎯

PURPOSE

Why was the information created? Inform, persuade, entertain, or sell?

🧾

EVIDENCE

What facts, data, documents, or reliable references support the claim?

🧠

CONTEXT

Does the information make sense when the surrounding context is examined?
